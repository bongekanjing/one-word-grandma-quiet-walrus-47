The single word my grandma would text about AI: **Poppycock**.

The whole joke is the mismatch. The most advanced machine in history arrives, and she answers it in
one syllable of Victorian disapproval. No amazement, no fear, no questions about whether it is
alive — just the word she has used on nonsense since 1962. It is warm rather than hostile: she is
not afraid of it, she simply is not impressed, and the machine has no reply.

One word, as the brief asks. Nine characters, well inside the thirty-character gate.

## Run it

```
node main.ts
```

The program writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `ENTRY_OUT`). It validates that
the text is between 1 and 30 characters and is not whitespace only, and exits non-zero otherwise.
